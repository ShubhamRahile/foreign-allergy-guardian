How It Works

When you scan a barcode or write ingredients, our AI Agent (Dietary Defender) runs standard matches against your allergy list.

We also cross-reference synonym patterns. For example: listing dairy will automatically alert you if the ingredients contain milk, lactose, whey, casein, or butter.
